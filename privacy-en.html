Block Lab privacy policy

Date: October 8, 2026. Android package: ru.gamezavod.blocklab.

Responsible publisher

Publisher and party responsible for data processing: Аблеев Дамир Рамилевич (Gibridq brand). App and privacy contact: gibrid-q-studio@yandex.ru.

Game data

The app stores your current game, score, personal records, tutorial progress, language and sound preference on your device to resume play and retain settings. Game code does not upload saves to a publisher server. There are no accounts, cloud leaderboards, purchases or separate publisher analytics.

Local data remains until you clear app data or uninstall the game. Starting a new game replaces the current game but retains records and settings. Android's “Clear data” action removes all local app data, including your progress.

Optional advertising

After losing, you may choose to continue by watching an ad. The app uses Yandex Mobile Ads SDK 8.5.0. Game code requests SDK initialization when you select this option and does not request ads during ordinary play. You can instead start a new game.

This version passes no consent for personalized advertising, enables age-restricted processing, and disables location tracking and app ad analytics reporting. These settings do not mean that the advertising service performs no network data processing. The game does not request access to location, camera, microphone, contacts or your files.

The SDK may process available device identifiers, including the advertising ID when permitted, and network information needed to deliver ads. Yandex services process this information under the Yandex privacy policy. The SDK documentation describes HTTPS/TLS transmission and provides SDK data disclosures.

INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE support ad loading and connectivity checks. The advertising dependency adds AD_ID permission. You can also manage advertising identifier restrictions in Android settings.

Support and external resources

If you email support, the publisher receives information you voluntarily include and uses it to respond. Do not send passwords or payment details. External links open resources governed by their own privacy rules.

Contact support with privacy questions or deletion requests. Requests about information held by Yandex may also be directed to its support channels. The publisher cannot promise to delete information from third-party systems directly.

Changes

Changes to SDKs or data handling require a dated policy update. The game has a general audience, including children. The SDK age-restricted data processing setting is enabled for all users. There is no separate child profile or request for a date of birth. The initial release is distributed in Russia.
