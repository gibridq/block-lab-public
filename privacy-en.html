Block Lab privacy policy

Updated November 8, 2026 for version 0.23.139. Android package ru.gamezavod.blocklab.

Publisher and data controller

The publisher and data controller is Аблеев Дамир Рамилевич (Gibridq). For questions about personal data, email gibrid-q-studio@yandex.ru.

Local play

You can play offline without registering. Games in progress, records, progress, your collection and settings are stored on your device. Android’s “Clear data” option removes local data, including your progress.

Accounts, cloud saves and leaderboards

Registration is optional and available to adults. With your consent, the account service processes your email address, password hash, account and session identifiers, profile nickname, consent records, game scores and cloud save. Email is used for account access and recovery. Session data and your network address help protect sign-in and the service. Game data is used to transfer progress and maintain leaderboards.

Connected Gibridq games share one account and keep separate leaderboards and saves. Anyone can view public ranks and scores. The table may include bot results. When names are hidden, a generic “Player” label appears in place of the profile name.

Retention and deletion

Account data is retained while consent is active and the account is in use. Withdraw consent in Account Management → Privacy or by emailing the data controller. Online account features are suspended. If you do not renew consent, automatic account deletion begins after 21 days.

You can start deletion immediately in Account Management. This removes the shared account and its online data across connected games. Local progress stays on your device. Backups containing account data are retained for up to five weeks. A technical deletion marker prevents deleted data from being restored from a backup.

You can email the data controller to request access, correction or deletion. The controller may verify that the request relates to your account. See the Gibridq account privacy policy for details in Russian.

Optional rewarded advertising

The Android version includes Yandex Mobile Ads SDK 8.6.0. Ads start only after you tap a button marked as advertising. A completed view can grant 25 crystals or 25 mosaic cubes. Offers are available after a game, in the mosaic menu and in the collection. You can play without watching ads. This version has no real-money payments.

The SDK preloads ads when preparing an offer, so requests to advertising servers may occur before you tap the watch button. To deliver and measure advertising, Yandex may process your IP address, technical information about your device, app and connection, technical identifiers available to the SDK, and information about ad requests, impressions and interactions. The game does not pass your Gibridq account email, nickname or password to the advertising module.

Age-restricted mode is enabled for all users, and the SDK is not told that consent to personalization has been obtained. Location sharing and additional ad analytics reports are disabled. The Android AD_ID permission is removed from the build. See the Yandex privacy policy for Yandex data processing, retention and data requests, and the Yandex Mobile Ads documentation for SDK data practices.

Offer availability times and reward receipts are stored on your device to track cooldowns and prevent duplicate rewards. Clearing the game's local data removes these records. Declining an offer does not block normal play.

RuStore, permissions and purchases

The game uses the RuStore SDK to request a rating. The build includes RuStore Core, Review and a RuStore metrics component. Ratings and reviews are optional. See the RuStore privacy policy for information about the store’s data processing.

Online features, the RuStore SDK and the Yandex advertising SDK use network permissions. You can turn off vibration in the game’s settings. The game does not request access to location, the camera, the microphone or contacts. Crystals and cubes are used only within the game and cannot be exchanged for money.

Support

If you contact support, the controller uses the information you include to respond to your request. Do not send passwords or payment details.
