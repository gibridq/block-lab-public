Block Lab — Google Play privacy policy

Privacy policy · Terms of use · Delete account

About this policy

Updated November 10, 2026 for the Google Play edition, starting with version 0.23.143. Android package: ru.gamezavod.blocklab. This page describes the Google Play edition; other store editions have separate policies.

Who is responsible

The publisher and data controller is Аблеев Дамир Рамилевич (Damir Ramilevich Ableev), operating under the Gibridq brand, Moscow, Russia. Contact for privacy, access, correction, withdrawal or deletion requests: gibrid-q-studio@yandex.ru.

Playing without an account

You can play without registration. Progress, games in progress, records, rewards, settings, the selected age group and advertising choice, and local review-prompt counters are stored on the device. A full date of birth is not requested. Clearing the app’s local data removes these files; signing out or deleting an online account does not erase local progress. Public leaderboard requests and opening website links can send your network address to the service you access.

Optional Gibridq account

Accounts are optional and available to people aged 18 or older. After separate account consent, Gibridq processes your email address, password hash, account and installation/session identifiers, profile nickname, consent records, game results and cloud save. These enable account access and security, optional email verification and password recovery, cloud progress and rankings. Requests reach our server over HTTPS; the network address is used to protect the service against abuse. We do not send your account email, password or nickname to the advertising module.

One Gibridq account is shared across connected games; their saves and leaderboards are separate. Showing your nickname and results publicly requires a separate, shared publication confirmation. Without it your personal scores are not published in the affected public leaderboards. Public tables can also contain clearly labelled bot results. The list of games and the data visible to guests are defined in the current shared nickname document, not by a separate Google Play consent.

Where account data is processed

The account database is hosted by FirstVDS in Moscow, Russia. Email delivery uses the Gibridq mail service and the recipient’s mail provider. Account access is restricted and passwords are stored as hashes. Using these optional online features transfers account data to Russia. Third-party services process their own data as explained in their linked policies.

Advertising choice

This edition is intended for older teenagers and adults. Advertising is disabled for users who select Under 18. Before optional advertising is enabled, an adult must select 18 or older and then explicitly allow reward videos. Back, declining or leaving the screen is not permission. All game modes remain available without ads. The choice is stored only on the device and can be changed in Settings → Ads and privacy.

Yandex Mobile Ads SDK 8.6.0 loads and displays optional rewarded videos. After permission, preparing an offer may start a network request before you tap the watch button. The video itself starts only when you choose to watch. A completed view can grant 25 crystals or 25 mosaic cubes. Offers appear after a round, in mosaics and in the collection. This edition has no real-money purchases.

To deliver and measure ads, Yandex may receive the IP address, technical device/app/network information, SDK or installation identifiers available under the restricted configuration, and ad request, impression and interaction information. Yandex Ads includes AppMetrica technical components (8.5.1 in this build); the game does not activate a separate AppMetrica app-analytics account. Personalization consent is not enabled in the SDK, age-restricted mode is enabled, location sharing and additional app-ad analytics reporting are disabled, and the Android AD_ID permission is absent. These restrictions do not mean that no technical data is processed.

Withdrawing permission blocks new ad loads and displays. Requests already started may finish; withdrawal cannot recall information already delivered to Yandex. Local offer deadlines and reward receipts prevent duplicate grants. For Yandex retention, data rights and deletion, see its privacy policy and contact mechanisms.

Google Play ratings and reviews

The edition uses Google Play In-App Review 2.0.2 through Gibridq Reviews. Google Play may offer an optional rating/review after a completed game. No reward or game access depends on leaving a review. The game stores only local prompt counters and timing; it cannot determine whether you submitted a review or read its stars or text through this API.

If you submit a rating or review, Google handles it using your Google Play account. It may be public, or shared privately with the developer in a closed test. Manage or delete it through Google Play or your Google Account. Deleting a Gibridq account does not delete a Google Play review. The Google Play edition does not include the RuStore rating plugin.

Retention, withdrawal and deletion

Account data is retained while consent is active and the account is used. Withdrawal in Account Management → Privacy suspends online account features. If consent is not renewed, automatic deletion begins after 21 days. You can request immediate deletion in Account Management → Delete account, or outside the app using the account deletion page linked below. This deletes the shared account and its online data across connected games, rather than only the Google Play installation.

Backups containing account data are retained for up to five weeks. A technical deletion marker prevents erased accounts from being restored from backups. Local progress remains on the device until you clear it. The account policy explains the shared consent and retention process. Third-party ad records, website access logs and Google reviews are managed by the corresponding providers under their own policies; no unverified universal deletion deadline is promised.

You may contact the controller to request access, correction or deletion, or exercise other rights applicable to your location. We may need to verify control of the account. You can withdraw optional consent without losing local play. Where applicable, you may also contact your competent data protection authority.

Permissions and support

The app uses internet/network access for online features, ads and Google Play integration, and vibration which can be disabled in Settings. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts or location permissions. Crystals and cubes are in-game items with no cash value. Support uses the contents of your message to respond; do not send passwords or payment details. Opening these policy pages uses GitHub Pages, whose host may process ordinary website connection information.
