Delete your Block Lab / Gibridq account

Privacy policy · Terms of use · Delete account

In the app

Open the player card → Account → Manage account → Delete account. Follow the sign-in/password confirmation shown by the app. This requests deletion of the shared Gibridq account and associated online data in all connected games, including Block Lab.

Outside the app

You can request account deletion without reinstalling Block Lab. Email gibrid-q-studio@yandex.ru from the address registered to the account, with the subject “Delete my Gibridq account — Block Lab”. State that you request deletion of the account and its online data. The email button below opens your mail app; press Send there to submit the request. If you cannot access the registered address, explain this to support so account ownership can be checked. Do not send your password.

What is deleted and retained

Deletion covers the account, sign-in sessions, profile, consent records, personal leaderboard entries and cloud saves across connected games. Local progress on your devices remains until you clear the app’s data. Backups may retain erased data for up to five weeks; a technical deletion marker prevents its return to the active service. Google reviews and Yandex advertising records are managed separately by those providers. Withdrawing consent without requesting immediate deletion starts the separate 21-day process described in the privacy policy.
